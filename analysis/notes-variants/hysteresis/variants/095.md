Hysteresis is a property observed in certain materials and systems where the output depends not only on the current input but also on the history of inputs. This means that the system's state depends on the path taken to reach that state, rather than just the current conditions.

Elements with Hysteresis:

Magnetic Materials: Many magnetic materials exhibit hysteresis, particularly ferromagnetic materials like iron, nickel, and cobalt. The magnetization of these materials depends on the history of magnetic fields applied to them.
Shape Memory Alloys (SMAs): These alloys, such as nickel-titanium (NiTi), can change shape in response to temperature changes, and their behavior can be hysteric.
Piezoelectric Materials: Some piezoelectric materials can show hysteresis in their electrical properties when subjected to mechanical stress.
Thermocouples: Certain types of thermocouples can exhibit hysteresis in their temperature readings due to the material properties and the way they respond to temperature changes.

Elements without Hysteresis:

Non-Magnetic Metals: Pure metals like copper, aluminum, and gold do not exhibit significant hysteresis because they do not retain a permanent magnetic field.
Semiconductors: Most semiconductors do not show hysteresis under normal operating conditions.
Insulators: Pure insulators do not exhibit hysteresis unless they are subjected to specific conditions that induce magnetic behavior (e.g., through doping or impurities).

Air:

Air itself does not have hysteresis. It is a gas and does not retain any memory of past states in the way that materials with hysteresis do. However, air can exhibit some hysteretic behavior in certain contexts, such as in the case of phase transitions (like condensation and evaporation) or in the context of thermal hysteresis (where the temperature at which a phase transition occurs can depend on the cooling or heating rate).

In summary, hysteresis is a property of certain materials and systems, and it is not something that applies to air in the same way it applies to magnetic materials or shape memory alloys.
