What has hysteresis? Elements etc. What does not have hysteresis? Does air have hysteresis?

Hysteresis is a property observed in certain materials and systems where the output depends not only on the current input but also on the history of inputs. This means that the system's state at any given time depends on its past states as well. Here are some examples and non-examples of hysteresis:

Examples of Hysteresis:
Magnetic Materials: When a magnetic material is exposed to a magnetic field, it can retain some magnetization even after the field is removed. This is known as remanence.
Rubber and Elastic Materials: These materials exhibit hysteresis in their stress-strain curves, meaning the energy required to deform the material is not fully recovered when the load is removed.
Thermocouples: The voltage generated by a thermocouple can depend on the temperature history of the material.
Saturable Inductors: In electrical circuits, the inductance of a saturable inductor can change depending on the previous current through it.

Elements That Do Not Have Hysteresis:
Non-magnetic Metals: Elements like copper, aluminum, and gold do not exhibit magnetic hysteresis because they are not ferromagnetic.
Non-Elastic Materials: Pure metals or alloys that do not deform elastically (like glass) do not show hysteresis in their mechanical properties.
Simple Electronic Components: Resistors, capacitors, and diodes typically do not exhibit hysteresis under normal operating conditions.

Air:
Air itself does not have hysteresis. It is a simple gas and does not retain any memory of past pressure or temperature changes in the way that materials with hysteresis do. However, air can exhibit some forms of hysteresis indirectly, such as in the case of air humidity, which can depend on the past conditions of temperature and pressure. But this is more about the behavior of water vapor in air rather than a direct property of air itself.

In summary, hysteresis is a characteristic of specific materials and systems, particularly those that are ferromagnetic or elastic. Air, being a simple gas, does not exhibit hysteresis.
