What has hysteresis? Elements etc. What does not have hysteresis? Does air have hysteresis?

Hysteresis is a property observed in certain materials and systems where the output depends not only on the current input but also on the history of inputs. This means that the system's state at any given time is influenced by its past states.

Elements with Hysteresis:

Magnetic Materials: Many ferromagnetic materials exhibit hysteresis, particularly when they undergo repeated magnetization cycles. Examples include iron, nickel, and cobalt.
Shape Memory Alloys (SMAs): These alloys can change shape in response to temperature changes and retain their new shape until the temperature is changed again. They exhibit hysteresis in their stress-strain curves.
Piezoelectric Materials: While primarily known for their ability to convert mechanical stress into electrical energy and vice versa, some piezoelectric materials can show hysteresis in their response to applied forces.

Elements without Hysteresis:

Linear Systems: In linear systems, the output is directly proportional to the input, and there is no memory of past inputs. Simple resistors, capacitors, and inductors in an ideal circuit behave linearly and do not exhibit hysteresis.
Ideal Springs: An ideal spring follows Hooke's law, where the force is directly proportional to the displacement. There is no hysteresis in such a system.
Ideal Capacitors and Inductors: These components store energy in electric and magnetic fields, respectively, and do not exhibit hysteresis.

Air and Hysteresis:

Air itself does not exhibit hysteresis because it is a simple gas and does not retain any memory of past states. The properties of air, such as pressure and volume, follow simple relationships described by the ideal gas law, which are linear and do not depend on the history of the system.

In summary, hysteresis is a characteristic of certain materials and systems, particularly those that can be magnetized or deformed in a way that retains a "memory" of past states. Air, being a simple gas, does not exhibit hysteresis.
