What has hysteresis? Elements etc. What does not have hysteresis? Does air have hysteresis?

Hysteresis is a property observed in certain materials and systems where the output depends not only on the current input but also on the history of inputs. This means that the system's state will "remember" past conditions even after those conditions have changed.

Examples of Hysteresis:
Magnetic Materials: The magnetization of ferromagnetic materials like iron, nickel, and cobalt depends on the history of magnetic fields applied to them. This is why these materials exhibit hysteresis loops when plotted as magnetization vs. magnetic field strength.
Rubber and Polymers: These materials can show hysteresis in their stress-strain curves, meaning the strain does not return to its original value when the stress is removed.
Saturable Inductors: In electrical circuits, saturable inductors can exhibit hysteresis in their inductance versus current characteristics.
Thermocouples: Some thermocouples can show hysteresis in their temperature response due to changes in the material properties with temperature.

Elements That Do Not Have Hysteresis:
Non-Ferromagnetic Metals: Elements like copper, aluminum, and gold do not exhibit hysteresis because they do not retain a permanent magnetic field.
Non-Saturable Materials: Materials that do not become permanently magnetized or deformed do not show hysteresis.
Linear Systems: Systems that follow linear relationships between input and output (like simple resistors) do not exhibit hysteresis.

Air:
Air itself does not have hysteresis. It is a non-saturated, non-magnetic medium and does not retain any memory of past conditions. However, if you consider the behavior of air in a system, such as its pressure and volume in a compressible fluid system, it can exhibit hysteresis-like behavior under certain conditions, especially in cyclic processes involving compression and expansion. But this is more about the system's behavior rather than a property of air itself.

In summary, hysteresis is a characteristic of specific materials and systems, particularly those that can be magnetized or deformed in a way that retains a memory of past conditions. Air, being a simple gas, does not inherently exhibit hysteresis.
