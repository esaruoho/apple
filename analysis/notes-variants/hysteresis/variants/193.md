What has hysteresis? Elements etc. What does not have hysteresis? Does air have hysteresis?

Hysteresis is a property observed in certain materials and systems where the output depends not only on the current input but also on the history of inputs. This means that the system's state will "remember" past conditions even after those conditions have changed.

Elements with Hysteresis:
Magnetic Materials: Many ferromagnetic materials exhibit hysteresis, including iron, nickel, and cobalt. The magnetic field strength (H) and the magnetization (M) of these materials are related through a hysteresis loop.
Rubber and Polymers: Some rubber and polymer materials can show hysteresis in their stress-strain curves.
Some Ceramics: Certain types of ceramics can also exhibit hysteresis under specific conditions.

Elements without Hysteresis:
Linear Systems: Most linear systems do not exhibit hysteresis. These systems respond directly to the current input without retaining any memory of past conditions.
Ideal Capacitors and Inductors: In ideal electrical components, the charge or current is directly proportional to the voltage or the magnetic flux, respectively, without any hysteresis.
Non-Ferromagnetic Materials: Materials like copper or aluminum do not show significant hysteresis because they do not retain magnetic properties in the same way as ferromagnetic materials.

Air:
Air itself does not have hysteresis. It is a non-viscous fluid and behaves as a linear system in most practical applications. The pressure and volume relationship described by Boyle's law or the combined gas law does not exhibit hysteresis. However, in some complex systems involving air, such as in certain types of compressors or in atmospheric processes over long periods, there might be effects that could be loosely compared to hysteresis, but this is not a fundamental property of air itself.

In summary, hysteresis is primarily associated with certain materials and systems that can remember past conditions, while linear systems and simple fluids like air do not exhibit this property.
