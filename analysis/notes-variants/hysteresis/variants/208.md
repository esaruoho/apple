What has hysteresis? Elements etc. What does not have hysteresis? Does air have hysteresis?

Hysteresis is a property observed in certain materials and systems where the output depends not only on the current input but also on the history of inputs. This means that the system's state will "remember" past conditions even after those conditions have changed. Common examples include magnetic materials, some types of rubber, and certain soils.

Elements with Hysteresis:
Magnetic Materials: These materials exhibit hysteresis when they are exposed to varying magnetic fields. The magnetization of these materials depends on both the current magnetic field and the history of previous magnetic fields.
Rubber and Certain Polymers: These materials can show hysteresis in their stress-strain curves, meaning the deformation depends on the history of loading.
Soils and Some Granular Media: These can exhibit hysteresis in their stress-strain behavior under cyclic loading.

Elements without Hysteresis:
Linear Materials: Materials that follow Hooke's Law (F = kx) do not exhibit hysteresis. They return to their original state immediately after the force is removed.
Ideal Gases: In an ideal gas, the pressure-volume relationship is linear and does not depend on the history of changes.
Pure Liquids: Pure liquids generally do not exhibit hysteresis in their phase transitions unless they are undergoing complex processes like freezing or melting under non-equilibrium conditions.

Air:
Air itself does not exhibit hysteresis because it is a simple fluid and follows the laws of thermodynamics in a straightforward manner. The properties of air (such as pressure, temperature, and density) change in response to immediate conditions and do not "remember" past conditions. However, in certain complex systems involving air, such as in weather patterns or atmospheric dynamics over long periods, there might be emergent behaviors that could be loosely described as having some form of hysteresis, but this is not a fundamental property of air itself.

In summary, hysteresis is a characteristic of certain materials and systems, particularly those that are nonlinear or have memory effects. Air, being a simple fluid, does not exhibit hysteresis.
