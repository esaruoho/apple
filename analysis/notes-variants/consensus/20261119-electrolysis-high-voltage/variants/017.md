## Answer

**Short answer:** Conventional electrolysis is current-driven (Faraday's laws) — you pass DC current through an electrolyte, and the mass of gas produced is proportional to charge passed (coulombs). This is what industry calls "brute force": significant current, significant I²R heating, overpotential losses, typical system efficiencies 60–80%.

**High-voltage / pulsed / resonant electrolysis** is a different paradigm claimed by several inventors. The core assertion: water acts as a dielectric in a capacitor; applying high-voltage pulses (often at resonance) with minimal average current can split water with far less energy input per mole of H₂ than Faraday predicts. Key figures and patents:

| Inventor / Patent | Year | Core Claim |
|---|---|---|
| Nikola Tesla, US 568,177 | 1896 | Impulse DC / ozone apparatus — electrical ancestor of pulsed high-voltage water treatment |
| Stephen Horvath, US 3,980,053 | 1974 | First water-electrolysis patent explicitly using high-voltage pulsed DC |
| Andrija Puharich | 1980s | High-frequency AC / complex waveforms |
| Stanley Meyer, US 4,936,961 et al. | 1988–1990s | "Voltage, not amperage, does the work"; resonant charging-choke circuit; water as dielectric of a capacitor; current falls toward leakage while voltage rises at resonance |
| Xogen / Paul Pendergrass | 1990s | Capacitive discharge into water |

**Meyer's own words (verified ledger):**
- "the voltage is increased and decreased but the current is maintained constant at a very low level" [Meyer-33]
- "because of that short condition it'll allow voltage potential around two volts so you can't go into resonance" [Meyer-257]
- "hence the arrangement is an inherent current limiting voltage source" [Meyer-39]

**Mechanism proposals (not all mutually exclusive):**
1. **Dielectric breakdown / field emission** at the electrode-electrolyte interface — high field strips electrons from water molecules directly.
2. **Resonant voltage multiplication** (Q-factor) in a series LC tank where the cell is the capacitor — kilovolts across the gap with milliamps average draw.
3. **Non-Faradaic charge separation** — the double layer is charged/discharged faster than ions can neutralize, producing gas without steady current.
4. **Plasma / cold plasma channels** in microbubbles — high-voltage pulses create transient plasma that dissociates water.

**Mainstream status:** No peer-reviewed replication has demonstrated sustained COP > 1 (energy out as H₂ HHV / electrical energy in) under controlled calorimetry. Conventional electrolysis remains the industrial standard. The high-voltage claims persist in the open-source / independent research community but lack the measurement rigor (simultaneous input power, gas volume, gas purity, calorimetry) that would convince a skeptical EE.

**What would settle it:** A single experiment running for ≥ 1 hour with:
- Calorimetric measurement of total electrical energy in (all ports: drive, bias, pump, control) — joules in
- Mass-flow or displaced-volume measurement of H₂/O₂ out — joules out (HHV)
- Gas chromatography confirming no contamination
- All instruments calibrated, data logged, no "black box" modules
If (joules out as H₂ HHV) > (joules in electrical) reproducibly, the claim stands. Until then, it remains an open experimental question.
